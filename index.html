Guest notes

Before you arrive.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is at 16:00 and check-out is at 10:00. If you arrive by train earlier, leave your bags with us and we will hold them.

Can I bring my bicycle?

Yes. Bicycles can be kept in the covered shed behind the house. We also have four bikes to borrow, free of charge.

Do you have parking?

There is free parking on the street in front of the house, and a car park two minutes away for longer stays.

Are children welcome?

Yes, and a cot can be set up in either room on request. Just tell us the ages of your children when you book.

Contact

Ask about your dates.

Send a note with your dates and the number of guests. We reply within one working day, usually the same afternoon.
